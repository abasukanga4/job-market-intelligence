# Market memo — UK data roles, 26 May – 10 Jun 2026

**To:** my own job-search strategy
**From:** the pipeline data (519 postings, Indeed + LinkedIn)
**Bottom line:** Target Data Analyst roles first, at 10× the application volume, filtered for direct employers. The data overturned my own plan.

---

## What the data says

**1. The volume is in Data Analyst, not Data Science.**
Data Analyst is the largest classifiable lane — 120 postings (23.1%) versus 69 Data Scientist (13.3%) and 86 ML/AI Engineer (16.6%). For a candidate transitioning from a compliance role with an MSc and a portfolio in progress, the realistic entry bar *and* the posting volume sit in the same lane: DA.

**2. Supply was never the constraint — my funnel was.**
In 16 days the pipeline surfaced **121 high-fit postings (fit score ≥ 70)**: 37 Data Scientist, 34 Data Analyst, 26 ML/AI. Over the same window I submitted **2 applications**, both into the harder DS/ML lanes. The bottleneck is not opportunity, and it is not capability-fit; it is application volume pointed at the wrong lane.

**3. Geography supports a Sheffield-based search.**
London dominates absolute volume (124), but Manchester (41), Bristol (27), Leeds (18) post steadily, and **42% of all postings are hybrid or remote** once the messy workplace field is normalized. A Yorkshire-based candidate is not locked out of this market.

**4. Raw feeds lie — ~14% is noise.**
The single most frequent "employer" is DataAnnotation (AI-training gig work, 36 postings), plus two recruiter aggregators (39 combined). Any scraped job feed needs a direct-employer filter before counts, scores, or alerts mean anything.

## What changes because of this

| Decision | Before | After (data-driven) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary target lane | Junior DS / ML Engineer | **Data Analyst** (DS as selective moonshots) |
| Application volume | ~1/week | **10–15/week from the high-fit queue** |
| Feed handling | Raw scrape | Direct-employer filter before scoring |
| Location filter | Remote-first | Remote + hybrid within Sheffield/Manchester/Leeds radius |

## Limitations

16-day window; two sources, Indeed-skewed (495/24); fit score is a personal LLM heuristic against my own CV, not a market-objective measure. Salary fields were too sparse to analyse — next pipeline iteration adds salary capture.
